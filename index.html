Installing the APK

You only need to do this once. Updates install the same way.

  1. Download the fileTap the download button on your Android phone. Chrome may ask you to confirm because it's an APK, so tap Download anyway.
  2. Open itTap the finished download in the notification bar or in your Files app.
  3. Allow this sourceIf Android asks, open Settings → Install unknown apps and allow your browser or Files app.
  4. Install and sign inTap Install, open Jarecut and sign in with your jarecut account. Then download the speech model once from Settings → Model & assets.
Tip: the first-time setup downloads about 670 MB (the speech model plus your music, SFX and B-roll pack). Use Wi-Fi and make sure you have a couple of GB free.
FAQ

Questions, answered.

Anything else? Ask whoever shared this page with you.

Is Jarecut free?
You can start a 5-day free trial right inside the app, which includes a daily export allowance. Pro accounts get unlimited exports.
Does it work offline?
Editing, transcription, face tracking and rendering all run offline once the speech model is downloaded. You only need a connection to sign in and for a quick plan check when you export.
Is my video uploaded anywhere?
No. Your videos stay on your phone. Captions are created on the device, and finished clips are saved to your gallery under Movies/jarecut.
Which phones are supported?
Any phone running Android 10 or newer with an H.264 video encoder, which covers almost every modern phone. A recent mid-range or flagship phone with 6 GB+ of RAM gives the smoothest 4K exports.
Why isn't it on the Play Store?
Jarecut is distributed directly as an APK for now. Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser the first time. You can check that the file is genuine by comparing its SHA-256 checksum with the one shown above.
Can I use my own music, sounds and B-roll?
Yes. Pick a folder on your phone that contains bgm, best hook, transitionsfx and broll subfolders, and Auto BGM, Hook SFX and Auto B-roll will use your files.
What video formats can I import?
Standard MP4 (H.264) works best. If a video is stored only in Google Photos or Drive, download it to the phone first.
